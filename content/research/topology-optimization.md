Traditional inverse design can be costly for dual-objective, multiphysics problems. We developed a dual neural network optimization algorithm to design a 3D thermal-electromagnetic shutter device using vanadium dioxide as a phase-change material.

A convolutional neural network acts as a surrogate model for device performance, while a second neural network optimizes the design subject to manufacturing limitations. The reduced computational cost enables efficient exploration of optimized design fronts.

Related publication: [A Dual Neural Network Approach to Topology Optimization for Thermal-Electromagnetic Device Design](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2023.103665).
