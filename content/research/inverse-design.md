Traditional inverse design can be computationally expensive for dual-objective, multiphysics problems. 
I develop data-driven optimization algorithms to accelerate the design of complex devices and fundamental material models.

**Topology Optimization:** I developed a dual neural network optimization algorithm to design a 3D thermal-electromagnetic shutter device utilizing vanadium dioxide as a phase-change material. This workflow uses a convolutional neural network as a surrogate model for device performance, while a second neural network optimizes the design subject to manufacturing limitations, significantly reducing computational cost.

**Interatomic Potentials:** I have extended these data-driven inverse design methods to the optimization of classical interatomic potentials.

Related publications: [A Dual Neural Network Approach to Topology Optimization for Thermal-Electromagnetic Device Design](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2023.103665) and [A data-driven method for optimization of classical interatomic potentials](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43580-024-00802-7).
