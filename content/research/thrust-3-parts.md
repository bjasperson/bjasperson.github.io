Real parts are rarely watched closely: measurements are sparse, and tests are few. This thrust brings models together with experiments, sensing, and engineering practice to estimate what is happening inside a specific part.

**Sensors and experiments:** My master's research developed thin-film heat flux sensors fabricated directly on copper pins for in-situ heat transfer measurements in micro-pin-fin heat sinks, including sensor design, fabrication with lithography and micro-end-milling, calibration, and laser-based testing.

**Design for fabrication:** I developed a dual neural network topology optimization method for a thermal-electromagnetic shutter device using vanadium dioxide, optimizing performance subject to manufacturing limits.

**Engineering practice:** I spent a decade as a mechanical design engineer on products ranging from healthcare devices to neutron generators, and I am a licensed Professional Engineer.

Related work: [Thin film heat flux sensors fabricated on copper substrates for thermal measurements in microfluidic environments](https://doi.org/10.1088/0960-1317/24/12/125018), [A Dual Neural Network Approach to Topology Optimization for Thermal-Electromagnetic Device Design](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cad.2023.103665), and [Devices and methods for delivering a beneficial agent to a user](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10213546B2/en) (U.S. Patent 10,213,546).
