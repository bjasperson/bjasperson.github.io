My master's research focused on integrated heat flux sensors fabricated directly on individual copper pins for in-situ heat transfer studies in micro-pin-fin heat sinks.

The work involved sensor design, sensitivity analysis, fabrication with lithography and micro-end-milling, calibration, and heat flux testing using an Nd:YAG laser system.

Related publication: [Thin film heat flux sensors fabricated on copper substrates for thermal measurements in microfluidic environments](https://doi.org/10.1088/0960-1317/24/12/125018).
